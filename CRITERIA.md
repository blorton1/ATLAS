# ATLAS sales prospecting criteria
Updated 2026-11-10 from Brendan's correction. This supersedes earlier Seed–Series D and research-first requirements.

ATLAS is a sales dashboard for identifying companies OpenAI could do business with, assessing commercial fit, and managing the path to a customer conversation.

Prioritize manufacturing companies and defense-tech startups. Include other potential OpenAI clients with a concrete, plausible AI use case. Retain US focus, but do not treat a narrow industry label or investor affiliation as the entire sales-fit decision.

Seed through later-stage companies are in scope. No Series D ceiling. Funding stage is context, not an automatic rejection rule. Public/acquired companies and unknown funding stages require a normal business-fit assessment rather than automatic exclusion.

For each prospect capture: company and sector; business need; proposed OpenAI workflow/product fit; potential buyer role; why now; suggested next sales action; and a reasoned High/Medium/Low/Unassessed priority. These are hypotheses until discovery confirms them. Show supporting links and uncertainties without making evidence administration the main workflow.

Daily automation should add potential clients for Brendan to review, with useful commercial briefs. No fabricated buying intent, customer status, budget or OpenAI spend. Preserve existing accounts, contacts, opportunities and activities. Only a human can approve qualification/promotion; discovery should not be held up by a missing funding-round announcement.
